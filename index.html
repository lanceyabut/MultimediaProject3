Hard Coral Collection

Hard Corals

Hard corals belong to the order Scleractinia and build rigid skeletons out of calcium carbonate. They are the main architects of coral reefs.

🌿

Soft Coral Collection

Soft Corals

Soft corals belong to the subclass Octocorallia and do not build hard calcium carbonate skeletons. Instead, they have flexible, fleshy, or leathery bodies supported by tiny skeletal needles called spicules.
